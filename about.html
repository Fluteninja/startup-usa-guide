About this guide

An independent, source-linked guide to federal and state funding for US startups.

Objective

This guide consolidates US federal startup programs — plus state-level incentives across all 50 states and DC — into a single, searchable reference. It is designed to help founders find the right program for their stage, sector, and situation.

Who it is for

  • Founders & Entrepreneurs
  • Students & Researchers
  • Immigrant founders navigating US visa pathways
  • Investors & Incubator staff

How to use it

  1. Start with the Scheme Finder. Answer 5 quick questions to find programs suited to you.
  2. Check the "What Do You Need?" Index for a need-based view (grant, loan, lab access, buyers, IP).
  3. Use the Lifecycle Map to find programs matching your stage of growth.
  4. Explore the full Directory for a structured overview of all programs with key details.
  5. Deep dive into Program One-Pagers for full details including how to apply.

Startup focus legend

Part A / Startup-Specific — startups are the primary or sole intended beneficiary; Part B / Startup-Relevant — broader eligibility (small businesses generally) but startups regularly benefit and apply.

How it is organised

The guide opens with an overview of federal programs (80 documented in full), a Scheme Finder wizard, a need-based index, and a Lifecycle Map. Beyond the federal layer, it links to federal-agency innovation programs (AFWERX, DIU, NSIN, In-Q-Tel and more), a state-by-state schemes index covering all 50 states + DC (65 programs), an incubator/resource-partner directory (SBA Small Business Development Centers, NSF I-Corps hubs, and accelerators) covering all 50 states + DC (111 entries), and every state's official economic-development portal. A dedicated section covers startup-visa and immigration pathways for founders who are not US citizens or permanent residents — a topic outside the scope of the India edition this project was adapted from.

The six types of support

GRANT

Fund given with no repayment needed, no equity given up.

LOAN / CREDIT

Repayable loan or credit guarantee to help access debt.

INCUBATION

Access to lab, workspace, mentoring and ecosystem support.

MARKET ACCESS

Pathways to sell, export, or access government procurement.

OTHER

Tax credits, exclusions, and fee reductions rather than direct funding.

MIXED

Combination of the above types of support.

About this website

This site is an independent reference compiled from official federal and state government sources — SBA.gov, SBIR.gov, Grants.gov, agency program pages, and each state's official economic-development portal. Every program page cites the specific official page it was drawn from, and every entry is re-checked against that source before it ships. There is no single source document the way there is for the India edition this project was adapted from; see the repository for the full sourcing method.

Licensing & attribution

Most content here describes US federal government programs. Works created by the US federal government are generally in the public domain under 17 U.S.C. §105 (unlike the India edition's source playbook, which is a Crown/Government copyright work reproduced here with attribution). State-program and private-accelerator content is summarized from each organization's own public materials, with a link back to the original source on every page.

Disclaimer

This site is an independent reference compiled to help founders find US federal and state programs relevant to startups. Unlike a single official government publication, it is compiled from many separate official sources — SBA.gov, SBIR.gov, Grants.gov, individual federal agency program pages, the IRS, USPTO, and each state's own economic-development portal. Program details — funding amounts, eligibility criteria, deadlines, and application windows — change frequently and are not controlled by this site. Every entry links to its specific official source page and is re-checked against that source before publishing, but drift after that point is expected and not something this site can catch automatically. This is a reference tool, not legal, tax, or financial advice, and not a government website. Before applying for, relying on, or structuring around any program described here — especially tax-driven ones like QSBS or Opportunity Zones — verify current details on the official government source linked from that program's page, and consult a qualified professional where the stakes warrant it. This project began as an adaptation of startup-india-guide (github.com/fritzhand/startup-india-guide), which documents India's central government startup schemes from a single official playbook PDF. That single-source model doesn't exist for the US, so this edition uses a different, multi-source verification approach described above.
