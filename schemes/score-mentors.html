SCORE Mentors (SCORE)

Small Business Administration (resource partner)

Other IdeationPrototype / PoCSeed / Early-StageGrowth / Scaling

SCORE is a nonprofit SBA resource partner, founded in 1964, that matches entrepreneurs at any stage with experienced volunteer mentors for one-on-one advice by phone, video, or email, alongside free workshops and templates.

SCORE — About ↗

Objectives

Increase small-business formation and survival by removing cost as a barrier to experienced business mentorship.

Who can apply?

  • Open to anyone starting, running, or exiting a small business — no revenue, stage, or industry restriction.

What do you get?

  • Free, ongoing one-on-one mentoring matched to your industry or challenge.
  • Free local and online workshops, templates, and business-planning tools.
  • 11 million+ entrepreneurs assisted since founding.

How to apply

Request a mentor through SCORE's website; matches are typically made within a few days.
