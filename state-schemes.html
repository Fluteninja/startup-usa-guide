State startup schemes

Every state runs its own economic-development incentives on top of federal programs — tax credits, seed funds, deal-closing grants, training reimbursements and more. State incentives stack with federal ones. All 50 states plus DC are covered here, each independently sourced from its official program page; a few flagged programs have sunset or paused for new applicants, and that status is noted where it applies.

State schemes

State incentives stack with federal programs — a startup can draw on both. Amounts, windows and eligibility come from each state's official economic-development portal and change often; blank fields are left blank rather than guessed. Always confirm on the state portal before applying. Spotted an error or a missing scheme? Open an issue on GitHub.
